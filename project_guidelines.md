# PROJECT GUIDELINE DOCUMENT
Dr. James R. Gordon Website
Domain: drjamesgordon.com

## 1. PROJECT OBJECTIVE
The website must position Dr. James R. Gordon as a top-tier, nationally recognized oculofacial plastic surgeon with deep clinical authority, refined aesthetics, and patient trust. The experience should feel luxury, medical-clean, and editorial, while remaining calm, credible, and conversion-focused.

**Primary goals:**
*   Establish authority and trust immediately.
*   Highlight awards, credentials, and outcomes.
*   Drive consultation bookings.
*   Maintain medical professionalism at all times.

## 2. DESIGN DIRECTION
**Overall Feel**
*   Luxury medical.
*   Editorial and refined.
*   Calm, confident, authoritative.
*   No flashy or gimmicky elements.

**Color Palette**
*   Neutral base.
*   Use the hexcodes exactly used on the GD layout.

**Typography**
*   Poppins Regular
*   Poppins Semibold
*   AzoSans Black
*   Bhineka
*   Headlines should feel authoritative, not marketing-heavy.

**Visual Language**
*   High-quality clinical photography.
*   Real patients only.
*   No stock imagery that looks generic.
